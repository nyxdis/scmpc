---
title: scmpc-0.5.0 has been released! And has moved again!
---

The first release in a long time, with many bug fixes. See `NEWS` and
`ChangeLog` for the full list. Highlights:

* scmpc builds with current compilers again (GCC 10 and later).
* Last.fm is now contacted over HTTPS.
* Password protected MPD servers work again.
* Duplicate scrobbles caused by unreadable Last.fm responses are fixed, and
  seeking no longer scrobbles a song twice.
* Many crashes are fixed, and the daemon detaches properly from the terminal.

This version requires glib-2.28. The project now lives at
[github.com/nyxdis/scmpc](https://github.com/nyxdis/scmpc), and downloads are
available from its [releases page](https://github.com/nyxdis/scmpc/releases).
