---
title: scmpc-0.5.1 has been released!
---

A bugfix release: if the first Last.fm login fails, for example because
scmpc starts before the network is up, scmpc now logs in again instead of
waiting for a restart. Thanks to Simon Arlott for finding this. Downloads are
on the [releases page](https://github.com/nyxdis/scmpc/releases).
